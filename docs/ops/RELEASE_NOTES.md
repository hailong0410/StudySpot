## Features

- Browse official Vanderbilt study spaces and open hours, room information and directions.
- Search and combine study-style and quiet-space filters.
- Share an observation of crowd and noise. The space page and search cards refresh the latest observation, show age, and distinguish empty or stale reports.

## Known limitations

- Observations are visitor-submitted. The app has no occupancy sensor integration.
- The anonymous reporting cooldown is per network address and space. Users sharing a network address may share the cooldown.
- The browse and report flow is public. Graders can open the production URL without an account. Vanderbilt login and moderation are pending scope confirmation.
